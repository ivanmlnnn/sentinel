WALLET SETUP / QUICK GUIDEConnect your NEAR account
01 · MetaMask

On a desktop browser with the MetaMask extension, install the official NEAR Protocol Snap first. Then tap Connect NEAR / MetaMask and choose MetaMask Snap in the wallet list. MetaMask Snaps are not supported in the MetaMask mobile app.

02 · Mobile fallback

On Android/iPhone, use HERE Wallet or Meteor Wallet from the wallet picker instead. MetaMask mobile cannot provide the NEAR Snap connection yet; a MetaMask EVM address alone is not a NEAR account.

03 · Run a scan

After connection, your NEAR account should appear in the Sender field. Paste the destination NEAR account, enter the amount if relevant, then tap Run inspection. This MVP only reads data; it never asks you to sign or send funds.
