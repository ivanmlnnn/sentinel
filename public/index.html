GET STARTED / 30 SECONDSThree simple steps
01 · Connect a wallet

Tap Connect NEAR Wallet and choose a wallet from the NEAR connector list. On mobile or desktop, choose a wallet that appears in the connector list for NEAR Testnet. Not every wallet supports Testnet. A MetaMask EVM address alone is not a native NEAR account.

02 · Check the account

Your real NEAR account ID appears in the Sender field after connecting. Only choose a wallet shown in the NEAR connection window.

03 · Inspect safely

Enter the destination NEAR account and tap Check address. Sentinel reads public testnet data. It never asks for your seed phrase, private key, or a transaction signature.
